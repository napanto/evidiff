### Privacy notice

*Template. Replace every part in square brackets before you run a study, and have the notice checked for your jurisdiction.*

**1. Who processes your data.** The controller is [name of the person or organisation running the study]. Contact: [contact address].

**2. Who can take part.** [Eligibility requirements of the study.] By starting, you declare that you meet them. Answers from people who do not meet them are not used.

**3. What we collect.**

- **Your answers** about the pairs of abstracts, with date, time and time spent.
- **At the end:** degree programme and year of study, as set in the site configuration.
- **A personal code.** The server keeps only a cryptographic fingerprint of it and a short hint to recognise it.
- **Technical data:** the server logs record your IP address and the page requested. To limit abuse, the site also keeps a cryptographic fingerprint of your IP address, not the address itself.

We do not ask for your name, email, phone number or any health data.

**4. Why we process them, and on what legal basis.**

| Purpose | Data | Legal basis |
|---|---|---|
| Carrying out the study: collecting and analysing the answers | Answers, times; programme and year in aggregate | Consent, given by pressing "Start" after reading this notice. You can withdraw it at any time; withdrawal does not affect processing already carried out |
| Site security and abuse prevention | Server logs, IP fingerprint | The controller's legitimate interest |

**5. Quality checks.**

- To check that the pairs are read carefully, the site measures the time per answer and compares your answers with those of other people who read the same pairs.
- If the checks flag answers that are very fast or random, the site may stop assigning you new pairs.
- No automated decision with legal effects is taken about you. You can ask for a person to review a flag.

**6. Who receives your data.**

- Your data stay on a server run by [hosting provider, country].
- [Any processor that handles the traffic, for example a content delivery network, and the safeguards for transfers.]
- Identifying data are not shared with anyone.
- Answers, in pseudonymised or aggregate form, are used in [the study's reports or publications].
- Your answers are **not sent to any artificial-intelligence service**.

**7. How long we keep them.**

- **Answers:** [retention period].
- **Server logs:** [retention period].
- **IP fingerprints:** at most 24 hours.

**8. Cookies and browser storage.** The site uses **only technical tools** needed for the service you request. It uses no profiling cookies, analytics or third-party content.

| Name | Type | Purpose | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|
| `participant` | Technical cookie (HttpOnly, Secure, SameSite=Lax) | Resume your session | 60 days |
| `me-lang`, `me-theme` | Browser storage | Remember your language and theme | Until you clear them |
| `me-draft-…` | Browser storage | Keep the answer you are writing | Deleted after submission |
| `me-code-…` | Browser storage | Remember your personal code on this device | Until you clear it |

You can delete cookies and storage in your browser settings. To resume your session you will then need your personal code.

**9. Your rights.** You can ask at any time to access, rectify or erase your data, to restrict its processing, to receive the data you provided in a readable format, to object to processing based on legitimate interest, and to withdraw your consent. Write to [contact address]. You can also lodge a complaint with your data protection authority.

**10. Do you have to provide your data?** No: participation is voluntary and you can stop at any time.
